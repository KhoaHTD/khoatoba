## Brand & Style

The design system translates the quiet rigor and tactile intimacy of analog Japanese stationery and educational workbooks (*drill-chō*) into a digital interface. It is crafted for language learners, educators, and scholars seeking disciplined, distraction-free study. 

The aesthetic is fundamentally tactile and editorial:
- **Paper Realism over SaaS Utility:** The interface behaves like physical sheets of aged *washi* (rice paper) marked with *sumi* ink. Surfaces are defined by paper rules, hairline score marks, and manuscript grids rather than drop shadows or synthetic glassmorphic blurs.
- **Editorial Directness:** Floating cards and gratuitous nesting are strictly eliminated. Hierarchy is established through typography, physical ruled dividers, and structural manuscript grids (*genkouyoushi*).
- **The Sensei’s Hand:** The human touch enters via the Vermilion *Akapen* (red correction pen)—deliberate, warm, and authoritative. It behaves as a physical marking tool: grading circles (*hanamaru* / *maru*), corrective strikethroughs, and hanko seals that ground the interface in physical pedagogy.

## Colors

The palette operates under rigorous discipline, mirroring natural stationery materials:

- **Canvas Background (`#F5F1E8`):** Aged rice-paper cream. Sets the organic, non-glare resting plane for long reading and writing sessions.
- **Paper Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure writing paper, reserved for deliberate input sheets, reading panes, and worksheet plates.
- **Sumi Ink (`#1C1C1E`):** Primary text and structural strokes. It retains the deep warmth of dissolved carbon ink, avoiding the sterile harshness of `#000000`.
- **Graphite Pencil (`#706C61`):** Secondary text, furigana readings, placeholder hints, metadata, and stroke-order guides.
- **Ruling / Border Line (`#D8D2C3`):** 1px structural guidelines, margins, and paper separation rules.
- **Genkouyoushi Grid (`#EAE4D5`):** Ultra-faint 1px interior cell guidelines for manuscript squares and writing alignment.
- **Akapen Vermilion (`#B23A2E`):** The primary and sole assertive accent color. Emulates traditional cinnabar/vermilion corrective ink. Used for active interactive states, primary action anchors, teacher annotations, and semantic error/re-study marks.
- **Koke Moss (`#5C7A5A`):** A muted, earthy desaturated green used exclusively for discreet correct-state verification before the vermilion evaluation mark takes visual precedence.

## Typography

The typographic pairing honors classical Japanese literary typesetting:

- **Noto Serif** anchors titles, literary prompts, kanji focal characters, and hanko stamps. Its brush-stroke terminal cuts mirror traditional chiseling and woodblock impression.
- **Noto Sans** carries functional body copy, questions, grammar breakdowns, furigana, and interactive controls. It provides clarity without modern corporate sterile geometry.
- **Ruby Text / Furigana:** Explicitly handled at `0.6875rem` (`furigana`) aligned directly centered over kanji clusters, colored in Graphite (`#706C61`) to avoid competing with reading flow.
- **Line Heights:** Generous line heights (up to `1.875rem` on body copy) accommodate Ruby characters cleanly without causing overlapping vertical collisions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a **Fixed Sheet Grid** simulating a physical desk pad or binding:

- **Desktop Layout:** Center-bound sheet layout constrained to a maximum content container of `960px` (single sheet) or `1280px` (open workbook spread). Uses an 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Mobile Layout:** Collapses to a 4-column column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer page margins, treating the screen as a pocket notebook (*techo*).
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Spacing operates strictly on a `0.5rem` (8px) grid cadence. Horizontal rules (`1px solid #D8D2C3`) structure vertical progressions between prompt modules, exercise headers, and notation areas. Whitespace is generous, invoking the Japanese concept of *Ma* (negative space as deliberate rest).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects conventional software drop shadows and heavy z-axis layering. Depth is expressed as **Physical Flatness and Paper Tiers**:

- **No Blurred Shadows:** Do not use `box-shadow: 0px 4px 20px rgba(...)`. Objects resting on washi do not cast synthetic ambient digital halos.
- **Tonal Planes:** The only elevation transition exists between the cream desk background (`#F5F1E8`) and the paper exercise sheet (`#FFFFFF`), separated exclusively by a crisp `1px solid #D8D2C3` line.
- **Deboss & Ruling:** Inactive inputs, writing guides, and genkouyoushi grids sit flush on the surface, etched using `#EAE4D5` hairlines.
- **The Akapen Layer:** The only elevated element is the vermilion layer, treated visually as wet ink placed directly atop the paper fiber with SVG drawn paths, circular stamps, and under-scores.

## Shapes

The shape system evokes trimmed sheets of fine paper, notebook edges, and carved wooden stamps:

- **Standard Radius (8px / `0.5rem`):** Applied to worksheet borders, exercise container sheets, and framed dialogs. Mimics the rounded cut corners of durable pocket notebooks.
- **Sub-element Radius (4px / `0.25rem`):** Applied to chip tags, character blocks, and individual genkouyoushi square cells.
- **Hanko / Stamp Forms:** The signature vermilion verification stamps use either strict square seals (`2px solid #B23A2E` with 2px corner radius) or natural circular rings (`maru`).
- **Never Pill-Shaped:** Rounded pill capsules (`9999px`) are forbidden; buttons and pills must maintain the discipline of cut paper corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Akapen Brush):** Solid `#B23A2E` background, `#FFFFFF` text, 8px corner radius, no drop shadow. On hover, shifts to `#9C2F24` with a sharp `1px` offset border simulating ink bleed.
- **Secondary Action (Sumi Outline):** Transparent surface, `1px solid #D8D2C3`, `#1C1C1E` sumi ink text. On hover, border transitions to `#706C61` with subtle background tint of `#F5F1E8`.
- **Text Button (Pencil Annotation):** `#706C61` typography with a subtle `1px solid transparent` bottom border that underlines in `#B23A2E` on hover.

### Genkouyoushi Input & Writing Areas
- **Grid Manuscript Box:** A container styled with `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #D8D2C3` outer perimeter.
- **Internal Ruling:** Subdivided into square cells using `1px solid #EAE4D5`. Each square features an ultra-fine dotted crosshair (`rgba(216, 210, 195, 0.5)`) dividing quadrant lines for stroke proportioning.
- **Active Focus:** Focus shifts the outer boundary from `#D8D2C3` to `#B23A2E` without fuzzy browser focus rings.

### Text Fields & Single Inputs
- Clean paper-rule style: A flat surface with a prominent `1px solid #D8D2C3` bottom stroke instead of a box container, or a crisp 8px framed box on white sheets. Placeholder text rendered in graphite `#706C61`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Selection Box:** 18px square or circle with `1px solid #D8D2C3` border and `#FFFFFF` fill.
- **Checked State:** Never a generic digital tick. Display an ink-drawn vermilion check mark or diagonal slash in `#B23A2E`.

### Teacher's Correction Marks (Akapen Maru & Hanko)
- **Hanamaru / Maru (Correct):** An SVG ring in vermilion `#B23A2E` overlaid directly above the answer box, emulating a freehand brush circle.
- **Correction Line / Cross (Incorrect):** A single vermilion `#B23A2E` strike or check, accompanied by corrective notes written in Graphite `#706C61` or Vermilion `#B23A2E`.
- **Hanko Stamp (Completion Grade):** Square seal enclosing kanji characters like *Yoku Dekimashita* (よくできました) or *Ken* (検) enclosed within a `2px solid #B23A2E` box, rotated slightly (`-2deg` to `2deg`) to honor physical stamping.

### Chips & Vocabulary Badges
- `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #D8D2C3` border, 4px corner radius, `label-sm` typography in `#706C61`. Category anchors use a small 4px square vermilion dot.

### Exercise Cards & Sections
- Eradicate floating SaaS cards. Delimit exercise modules via continuous white paper panels separated by `1.5rem` gaps on the `#F5F1E8` desk canvas, bound by `1px solid #D8D2C3` borders and neat header rulings.
